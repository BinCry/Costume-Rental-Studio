# payOS Phase 2 completion report

## Summary

Public payment creation now has two explicit paths: employee-recorded `CASH` and payOS
`PAYMENT_GATEWAY`. Manual `BANK_TRANSFER` creation/confirmation is no longer exposed.

| Terminal-order or mismatched provider events require review | Complete |
